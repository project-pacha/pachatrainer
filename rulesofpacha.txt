# Loan finder
Transliteration. ISO 15919

roots or stem (mostly verbs) are marked with - (example śēkhari- from which forms such as śēkhariccu, śēkharikkuka, śēkharikkunna, śēkharicca etc comes)
The hypen (-) also indicates that words or letters might join in its place (its position may be -x before or x- after or -x- the target being inside)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Identifying loan words
1.1 Words having Mahaprana letters: kh, gh, ch, jh, ṭh, ḍh, th, dh, ph, bh are all Sanskrit loan words with exceptions being viḍḍhi-, śēkharaṁ, śēkhari-

1.2 Words with g, j, ḍ, d, b, ṣ are likely loans from Sanskrit or other languages with exceptions being daśa, daśanārŭ, gaḍi, gaḍḍi, guṇṭŭ, viḷambu-; words with s are likely loan words except srāvŭ, taṭassaṁ, urasuka, urasal

1.3 r̥ (letter or sign), ḥ (letter or sign) are Sanskrit loans with no exceptions

1.4 Words having consonant clusters: ḻs, sṯṯ, sk, bl, br, ḍr, phl, phr, gl, skr, sṭr,  ṭv, kc, kṭ and consonant-vowel clusters: isaṁ, kla, kli, kle, klō, krō, krē,  ṭra, ṭri, ṭrō, ṯṯai are likely english loan words.

1.5 Words having consonant clusters ky, cy, ṭy, ṇy, ty, ny, py, my, ry, ly, vy, śy are Sanskrit loan words 

1.6 Words having consonant clusters kv, cv, ṭv, ṇv, tv, nv, pv, mv, rv, lv, śv are Sanskrit loan words

1.7 Words having consonant clusters kt, kn, kp, km, kr (except mākri, pōkri), kl (except klāvŭ), kś, ṅm, ṅr, ṅl, ṅḷ, ct, cn, cm, cr, cl, ñk, ṭk, ṭṇ, ṭn, ṭp, ṭr, ṭc, ṅc, pt, pr (except for prāvŭ), pl (except for plāvŭ, plāmpaḻaṁ), mt, mn, mr, ml (except for mlāvŭ), śk, śc, śṇ, śp, śn, śm, śl, śr are likely sanskrit loan words  

1.8 Sanskrit derived tense markers: -karikku, -kariccu, -karikka, -karikkuka -valkkari-
1.9 Sanskrit derived nominal markers: -tvam/-tva-, -karaṇaṁ/-karaṇa-, -ata, -ryam/-rya-, -ikaṁ, -vān/-mān, -vat/-mat, -vati/-mati, -īyam, -mayaṁ, -kāran/-kāri (can be marked as tadbhava), -vādi, -jñan, -tāvŭ, -paraṁ, -pūrṇaṁ, -śīlaṁ, -ātmakaṁ, -itaṁ, -anīyaṁ, -tyaṁ, -tamaṁ
1.10 Sanskrit derived adjectival markers: -maya, -ika, -īya, -para, -paraṁāya, -pūrṇa, -śīla, ātmaka, -vattāya/-mattāya, -ita, -anīya, -tya, -tavya, -tama,

# 2. Isolating the stem: sandhi and case
Rules for isolating stem/root from sandhi and case markers

2.1 Sandhi
2.1.1 -āmalla → -āṁ + alla; -āmilla → -āṁ + alla; 
2.1.2 Before any vowel-initial suffix, final ṁ becomes m (kalāṁ + ine → kalāmine)
2.1.3 Words ending in chillu l and ṇ preceeded by short vowel will have their last letter duplicated when a suffix with vowel is succeeded. (kal/kaṇ -> kalluṁ, kaṇṇuṁ, kallumāyi, kaṇṇumāyi, kallumottŭ, kaṇṇumottŭ, kallilninnŭ, kaṇṇilninnŭ, kallāyi, kaṇṇāyi, kallinuvēṇṭi, kaṇṇinuvēṇṭi, kallāṇŭ, kaṇṇāṇŭ‌)
2.1.4 Words ending in -ai will have the -ai replaced with -ayy- when a suffix with vowel is succeeded. (kai -> kayyuṁ, kayyumāyi, kayyōṭŭ, kayyumottŭ, kayyilninnŭ, kayyāyi, kayyāṇŭ)

2.2 Case and Plural endings by final sound of stem: stem ends in;

2.2.1 -aṁ [example maraṁ]
	accusative: -tte (maratte)
	dative: -ttinŭ (marattinŭ)
	instrumental: -ttāl (marattāl)
	comitative: -ttōṭŭ (marattōṭŭ)
	genitive: -ttinṟe (marattinṟe) never uṭe
	locative: -ttil/-ttiṅkal (marattil/marattiṅkal)
	vocative: -mē (maramē)
	additional locative: -ttinmē/-ttinmēl (marattinmē/marattinmēl)
	additional locative: -ttinaṭiyil (marattinaṭiyil)
	additional dative: -ttināyi/-ttināy (marattināyi)
	
	all nouns ending in -aṁ are non-gendered, hence their plural suffix is -ṅṅaḷ by deleting the -ṁ (maraṅṅaḷ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2.2 -āṁ / -īṁ / -ūṁ/ -ŭ/chillu ṇ (pure ṇ) preceeded by long vowel [example kalāṁ]
	accusative: -ine (kalāmine)
	dative: -inŭ (kalāminŭ)
	instrumental: -āl (kalāmāl)
	comitative: -ōṭŭ (kalāmōṭŭ)
	genitive: -inṟe (kalāminṟe) never uṭe
	locative: -il/-iṅkal (kalāmil/kalāmiṅkal)
	vocative: -ē (kalāmē)
	additional locative: -inmēl (kalāminmēl)
	additional locative: -inaṭiyil (kalāminaṭiyil)
	additional dative: -ināyi/-ināy (kalāmināyi)
	
	if nouns ending in -āṁ / -īṁ / -ūṁ is a person's name, then the plural suffix is -umār (kalāmumār), otherwise, the plural suffix is -ṅṅaḷ by deleting the -ṁ (vālīṁ -> vālīṅṅaḷ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------	
2.2.3  -a/-i /-ī [example tala]
		accusative: -ye (talaye)
		dative: -ykkŭ (talaykkŭ)
		instrumental: -yāl (talayāl)
		comitative: -yōṭŭ (talayōṭŭ)
		genitive: -yuṭe (talayuṭe) never -inṟe
		locative: -yil/-yiṅkal (talayil/talayiṅkal)
		vocative: -yē (talayē)
		additional locative: -yinmēl (talayinmēl)
		additional locative: -ykkaṭiyil (talaykkaṭiyil)
		additional dative: -ykkāyi/-ykkāy (talaykkāyi)
		
		If noun is gendered then the plural suffix is -amār for -a (anilamār); -imār for -i (taruṇimār); -īmār for -ī (śālīmār). Otherwise, the plural suffix is -kaḷ (talakaḷ, kaṟikaḷ, mānīkaḷ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------		
2.2.4 -ā/ -u / -ū/ [example nilā]
		accusative: -vine (nilāvine)
		dative: -vinŭ (nilāvinŭ)
		instrumental: -vāl (nilāvāl)
		comitative: -vōṭŭ (nilāvōṭŭ)
		genitive: -vinṟe (nilāvinṟe)
		locative: -vil/-viṅkal (nilāvil/nilāviṅkal)
		vocative: -vē (nilāvē)
		additional locative: -vinmēl (nilāvinmēl)
		additional locative: -vinaṭiyil (nilāvinaṭiyil)
		additional dative: -vināyi/-vināy (nilāvināyi)
		
	If noun is gendered then the plural suffix is -āmār for -ā (śīlāmār); -umār for -u (anumār); -ūmār for -ū (tānūmār). Otherwise, the plural suffix is -kkaḷ for -ā, -u and -ū (nilākkaḷ, tanukkaḷ, pūkkaḷ); Sometimes, -ūvukaḷ appear for -ū (pūvukaḷ).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2.5 -ai [example kai] -ai is replaced wtih -a in all suffixations
	accusative: -yye (kayye)
	dative: -ykkŭ (kaykkŭ)
	instrumental: -yyāl (kayyāl)
	comitative: -yyōṭŭ (kayyōṭŭ)
	genitive: -yyuṭe (kayyuṭe)
	locative: -yyil/-yyiṅkal (kayyil/kayyiṅkal)
	vocative: -yyē (kayyē)
	additional locative: -yyinmēl (kayyinmēl)
	additional locative:-ykkaṭiyil (kaykkaṭiyil)
	additional dative: -ykkāyi/-ykkāy (kaykkāyi)
	
	If noun is gendered then the plural suffix is -mār (anavaimār). Otherwise , the plural suffix is -kaḷ (kaikaḷ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2.6 chillu -n (pure n) preceeded by short vowel [example avan]
	accusative: -e (avane)
	dative: -ŭ (avanŭ)
	instrumental: -āl (avanāl)
	comitative: -ōṭŭ (avanōṭŭ)
	genitive: -ṟe (avanṟe)
	locative: -il/-iṅkal (avanil/avaniṅkal)
	vocative: -ē (avanē)
	additional locative: -mēl (avanmēl)
	additional locative: -aṭiyil (avanaṭiyil)

 If noun is gendered then the plural suffix is -mār (avanmār). Otherwise , the plural suffix is -ṅkaḷ (kaṭaṅkaḷ)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------	
2.2.7 chillu -n (pure n) preceeded by long vowel [example mān]
	same rules as "2.2.6 chillu n (pure n) preceeded by short vowel (example avan)" except for the following changes;
	dative: -inŭ (māninŭ)
	genitive: -inṟe (māninṟe)
	additional locative: -ninaṭiyil (māninaṭiyil)
	additional dative: -ināyi/-ināy (mānināyi)

 If noun is gendered then the plural suffix is -mār (bhayavānmār). Otherwise , the plural suffix is -ukaḷ (mānukaḷ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2.8 chillu -ṇ (pure ṇ) preceeded by short vowel [example kaṇ]

	additional dative: -ṇināyi/-ṇināy (kaṇṇināyi)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2.9 chillu -r (pure r)/chillu -l (pure -l) preceeded by a long vowel/ chillu ḷ (pure ḷ) preceeded by long vowel  [examples pār & kār]
	accusative: -ine (pārine & kāṟine)
	dative: -inŭ (pārinŭ & kāṟinŭ)
	instrumental: -ināl (pārināl & kāṟināl)

	locative: -il/-iṅkal (pāril & kāṟil/pāriṅkal & kāṟiṅkal)

	additional locative: -inmēl (pārinmēl & kāṟinmēl)
	additional locative: -inaṭiyil (pārinaṭiyil & kāṟinaṭiyil)
	additional dative: -ināyi/-ināy (pārināyi & kāṟināyi)
	
There is no specific pattern when r or ṟ will appear, it needs to be marked in the lexicon : treat it as a per-lemma flag in the lexicon, and when stripping

If noun is gendered then the plural suffix is -umār for chillu -l (pure -l) preceeded by a long vowel/ chillu ḷ (pure ḷ) preceeded by long vowel. Otherwise, the plural suffix -ukaḷ (ālukaḷ, kālāḷukaḷ)

2.3 Suffix inventory - common suffixes for isolating stems/roots
for case markers + suffixes, suffixation rules mentioned above for case markers apply

2.3.1 -uṁ is 'and' (kalluṁ palluṁ = stone and teeth). 
2.3.1.1 In agglutination, the 'and' suffix will only come after the plural suffix is added eg: (kalluṁ kallukaḷuṁ = stone and stones: kal[noun] + ḷukaḷ [plural marker] + -uṁ[and marker); -ṅṅaḷuṁ is plural + and. 

2.3.2 -umāyi is 'with' (kallumāyi)

2.3.3 -umottŭ is 'together with'. (kallumottŭ) 

2.3.4 case marker: -ilninnŭ/-ilninnu is 'locative + engendering'. It is a combination of locative -il + ninnŭ/ninnu; -ilēkkŭ/-ilēkkuḷḷa is 'locative + towards'. it is a combination of locative -il + ēkkŭ/ il + ēkkuḷḷa

2.3.5 -āyi is 'becoming' (kallāyi)

2.3.6 -uvēṇṭi (dative + for) is a combination of dative -ŭ + vēṇṭi. Common variations include -uvēṇṭunna, -uvēṇṭātta, -uvēṇō, -uvēṇṭē, -uvēṇaṁ

2.3.7 -āṇŭ/-āṇu is copula 'is/are'. paramāṇu is a sanskrit loan.
2.3.7.1 for -aṁ/-āṁ / -īṁ / -ūṁ/ -ŭ/chillu ṇ (pure ṇ) preceeded by long vowel words ending nouns the suffix is -māṇŭ/-māṇu (maraṁ -> maramāṇŭ).
2.3.7.2 for -a/-i /-ī ending words the suffix is -yāṇŭ/-yāṇu (talayāṇŭ)
2.3.7.3 for -ā/ -u / -ū ending words the suffix is -vāṇŭ/-vāṇu (nilāvāṇŭ)
2.3.7.4 for -ai ending words the suffix is -yyāṇŭ/-yyāṇu (kayyāṇŭ) where -ai is replaced
2.3.7.5 for chillu -n (pure n) preceeded by short vowel/chillu -r (pure r)/chillu -l (pure -l) preceeded by a long vowel/ chillu ḷ (pure ḷ) preceeded by long vowel ending words the suffix is -āṇŭ/-āṇu (avanāṇŭ); chillu -ṇ (pure ṇ) preceeded by short vowel the suffix is -ṇāṇŭ/-ṇāṇu (kaṇṇāṇŭ); 
2.3.7.6 for chillu -l (pure l) preceeded by short vowel the suffix is -lāṇŭ/-lāṇu; for chillu -ḷ (pure ḷ) preceeded by short vowel the suffix is -ḷāṇŭ/-ḷāṇu

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3.8 Negative markers : -arutŭ, -alla, -illa, -pōra, -vēṇṭa are the negative markers and the above rules of suffixations apply 

2.3.9 affirmative markers: -uṇṭu/-uṇṭŭ, -vēṇaṁ are the affirmative markers in addition to -āṇŭ/-āṇu and the above  rules of suffixations apply 

2.4 Verb and Tense
Only works with verbs
2.4.1 -unnu is the present tense. verb root + -unnu. Most verb roots end in -u. So only -nnu is applied (root: tallu + -nnu = tallunnu); If verb root ends in chillu l or r then the suffix is -kkunnu (root: nil + -kkunnu = nilkkunnu); negative form of -unnu is -unnilla

2.4.2 -ukayāṇŭ/ukayāṇu is infinitive verb form. Same rules of -unnu applies for chillu l and r. Negative form of -ukayāṇŭ/ukayāṇu is -ukayāyirunnilla

2.5 Common native adjectival and participial markers
-āya(copular), -āyi(past adverbial particple), -uḷḷa(having/existing), -aṟṟa(lacking), -uṟṟa(containing), -illātta (not having), -ārnna (filled with), -enna (complementiser), -ākunna (becoming), -takka (suitable), -ākāvunna/-ākkāvunna (probable), -koṇṭuḷḷa (made with), -niṟañña (filled with), -keṭṭa (not having/lacking), -pōluḷḷa/-pōlatte (similar)
